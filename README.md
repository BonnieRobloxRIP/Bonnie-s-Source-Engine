After arriving at the Flood Facility and Electronics Center, you and your team must work together to push buttons, find the way out, crack the puzzles and escape before the flood takes you! Else, experiment concludes as failure.

In this personal take on the classic flood escape game by crazyblox on roblox, you and your friends must deal with much more serious challenges than what the original had to offer in even more immersive and unpredictable ways. beware how the game throws you off :)
